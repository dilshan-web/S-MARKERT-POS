⚡ Quick Menu & Operations (මෙනුව)

📱

Install LankaPOS App (ඇප් එක ස්ථාපනය කරන්න)

Install as a Native App on PC, Android & iPhone

🤖 Android (Google Chrome):
  1. Tap the 3 dots (⋮) menu in the top right of Chrome.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" (මුල් තිරයට එක් කරන්න).
  3. Open the app directly from your phone's home screen anytime, even 100% offline!
🍎 iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari):
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (⎙ / Share) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (මුල් තිරයට එක් කරන්න ➕).
  4. Tap "Add" at the top right. LankaPOS is now your standalone iPhone app!
💻 PC / Laptop (Chrome / Edge):
  1. Click the Install icon (⊕) in the right corner of your browser's address bar.
  2. Click "Install" to launch as a desktop standalone application with its own window and desktop shortcut.
